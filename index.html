First 90 days

Listen, design, prove

A plan written from outside, so it is a hypothesis. The first 30 days exist to correct it.

Days 1 to 30: Listen and diagnose

  • Meet Clinical, Operations, Quality & Safety, Product, and Data leaders. One question each: what frontline behavior would you change first?
  • Shadow frontline shifts, clinical and non-clinical. Listen to calls and read messages.
  • Inventory every course, job aid, and knowledge article. Who owns it, when was it last reviewed, who uses it.
  • Baseline the numbers: time to proficiency, QA scores, escalation accuracy, repeat contacts, patient experience.

Days 31 to 60: Design the operating model

  • Open one front door for learning requests, with the "what goes wrong if people don't learn this" question.
  • Agree the prioritization score with Operations and Quality, so the queue is a shared decision.
  • Set content governance: owner, clinical approver, review cadence, retirement rule.
  • Pick one known performance gap and diagnose it end to end.

Days 61 to 90: Ship and prove

  • Launch the fix for that one gap, whether it turns out to be training or not.
  • Publish measurement v1: the four-rung ladder for onboarding and the pilot.
  • Present the CoE design: roles, hiring plan, and the tech stack decision with a scorecard.
  • Run a lessons-learned review and change one thing in the model because of it.
