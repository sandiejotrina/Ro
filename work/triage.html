Is training the answer?

Five requests land in the Learning CoE's intake queue. Each comes with one thing you'd find if you dug in. Pick the fix. Training is right only some of the time.

The test behind every answer: could they do it if their job depended on it? If yes, it isn't a skill gap, so training won't fix it.

The five fixes

  • Training: new knowledge or skill that people need to perform without looking it up.
  • Job aid: steps people know but do rarely, or in a strict order.
  • Knowledge fix: the source of truth is wrong, missing, or contradicts itself.
  • Process or tool fix: people know what to do, and the system makes it hard.
  • Coaching: a few people, a clear standard, and missing feedback.

Why this matters at Ro's scale

Every course a team builds has to be maintained for as long as it exists. Sending a knowledge or tool problem to training costs build time, takes hundreds of people off the queue to complete it, and leaves the real cause in place. The intake form asks "What goes wrong if people don't learn this?" so the diagnosis starts before anyone builds anything.
